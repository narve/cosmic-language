magnetism and light

New: the domain wires (long, straight, parallel wires that carry a steady current), the functions current (the charge that flows along a wire per time) and magnetic (the magnetic force between two wires, per length of wire), and the constant km. Charges at rest feel the electric force; charges in motion also feel a second one, the magnetic force. Like the electric force, it is introduced through its law, and a negative force pulls together.


; a current is charge per time; its sign says which way it flows
; along the wire
(∀ u wires (= (dim (current u)) (÷ Q T)))
; a current divided by one charge: how many charges pass per time
(∀ u wires (= (dim (÷ (current u) (charge proton))) (÷ 1 T)))

; the law: magnetic force per length = −2 × km × current × current ÷
; distance
; so currents in the same direction pull the wires together (a force
; below 0)
; (only where the distance is above 0: a wire and itself are left
; out)
(observed (∀ u wires (∀ v wires
  (implies (> (distance u v) 0)
    (= (magnetic u v)
       (neg (÷ (× 2 km (current u) (current v)) (distance u v))))))))

; the dimension of a force per length: mass ÷ time²
(∀ u wires (∀ v wires (= (dim (magnetic u v)) (÷ M (^ T 2)))))
; the dimension of km, which makes the law come out that way
(= (dim km) (÷ (× M L) (^ Q 2)))

; each wire pulls the other with the same force
; (current, magnetic and km come back later, in Maxwell's equations
; of the electric and magnetic field)
(observed (∀ u wires (∀ v wires (= (magnetic u v) (magnetic v u)))))

Nothing in the electric law or the magnetic law is about light: one is about charges at rest, the other about currents in wires. Yet the ratio of their two constants has the dimension of a speed squared, and it is the speed of light:


; ke ÷ km has the dimension of a speed squared
(= (dim (÷ ke km)) (^ (dim c) 2))

; and it is the speed of light squared
; (Maxwell's equations, later in the message, explain why: light is
; a wave of the electric and magnetic field)
(observed (= (÷ ke km) (^ c 2)))

This is how it was discovered that light is electric and magnetic: the ratio was measured with charges and currents, long before anyone could explain why it should equal the speed of light.
