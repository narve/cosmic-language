large numbers

num writes a number with several digits in base 256, most significant digit first: (num 1 0) means 1·256 + 0. 


; (num 1 0) is 256, the number after 255; it is also 16 × 16
(= (num 1 0) (+ 255 1))
(= (num 1 1) (+ 255 2))
(= (num 1 0) (× 16 16))

; the first digit counts 256s, the second counts ones
(= (num 2 0) (× 2 (num 1 0)))
(= (num 1 5) (+ (num 1 0) 5))

(< 255 (num 1 0))
(> (num 2 0) (num 1 255))

; counting on past 255: 254, 255, 256, 257, 258
(< 254 255 (num 1 0) (num 1 1) (num 1 2))
; when the last digit is full, the next digit goes up: 511 + 1 = 512
(= (num 1 255) (+ (num 1 0) 255))
(= (+ (num 1 255) 1) (num 2 0))

; three digits: (num 1 0 0) is 256 × 256
(= (num 1 0 0) (× (num 1 0) (num 1 0)))

As bits, the two digits of (num 1 0) are the value chunks 00000001 and 00000000, each after its own type chunk. Put the two digits side by side and you have 256 in binary.
