Four Versions

The message comes in three versions, and a fourth made for fun. The three are made from the same lessons, by leaving whole lessons out; nothing is rewritten. They differ in how much they say about who sends them and where from.

Full

Every lesson, including those that say where the message comes from: our star and our planet, and later where they are in the Galaxy and who we are. It reveals as much as possible.

Files: message.txt, message.bits, message.bin, message.bin.txt.

Cautious

Everything except the lessons that reveal where and who we are: these are marked optional, and the build checks that no other lesson depends on them. Mathematics, logic, physics, chemistry, stars in general, the background radiation and the game are all there; our Sun and our Earth are not.

Files: message.txt, message.bits, message.bin, message.bin.txt.

Minimal

Only the game and what it needs: how the signal is read, arithmetic, truth values, lists, quantifiers, sequences, chance and if, and then the prisoner's dilemma, which ends with the statement that the strategy which opens in peace and answers in kind loses least. No lesson in it uses a symbol introduced outside it, and the build checks that.

Files: message.txt, message.bits, message.bin, message.bin.txt.

Its lessons

Decoy

The full message, with the lesson on the Sun replaced by one about Proxima Centauri, the nearest star, and its planet Proxima b, under the same names. It is made for fun, to see whether a blind reader places the sender there. Proxima is near enough that the lessons on the Galaxy and on pulsars hold as they are. Its measured ranges are not checked by the build, and it says false things about the sender, so it is not meant to be sent.

Files: message.txt, message.bits, message.bin, message.bin.txt. The replacing lesson: the Sun (decoy).

A note on numbering

The symbols keep the numbers they have in the full message, so a shorter version has gaps between them. A reader of an earlier minimal version took such a gap for a deliberate signal. Renumbering per version is a possible improvement.
