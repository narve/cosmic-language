counting

In bits, the start and the end of the lesson look like this:

00000000 00000000   0
00000000 00000001   1
00000000 00000010   2
00000000 00000011   3
00000000 00000100   4
…
00000000 11111110   254
00000000 11111111   255

Every other chunk is always 00000000 (the type), and the chunk in between goes up by one each time. In that chunk the rightmost bit flips at every step, the next bit every second step, the next every fourth, and so on, until the leftmost bit turns on at 128. That is the pattern of counting in binary. It shows the receiver that tokens are 16 bits long, that the value is a binary number with the most significant bit first, and that the count stops when all eight bits are set.
