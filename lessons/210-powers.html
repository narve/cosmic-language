powers

New symbol: ^, raising to a natural-number power. Like − and ÷ it takes exactly two arguments, and the lesson shows why: grouping matters.

(= (^ 2 3) (× 2 2 2) 8)  (= (^ 3 2) 9)  (= (^ 5 1) 5)  (= (^ 0 5) 0)  (= (^ 5 0) 1)
the first number that needs two digits
(= (^ 2 8) (big 1 0))
(= (^ (^ 2 3) 2) 64)  (= (^ 2 (^ 3 2)) (big 2 0))  (≠ (^ (^ 2 3) 2) (^ 2 (^ 3 2)))
(∀ u ℝ (= (^ u 2) (× u u)))
(∀ u ℝ (∀ v ℕ (= (^ u (+ v 1)) (× u (^ u v)))))
(∀ u ℝ (∀ v ℕ (∀ w ℕ (= (^ u (+ v w)) (× (^ u v) (^ u w))))))
