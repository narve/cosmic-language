large numbers

New symbol: big. It writes a number with several digits in base 256, most significant digit first: (big 1 0) means 1·256 + 0.

(= (big 1 0) (+ 255 1)) (= (big 1 1) (+ 255 2)) (= (big 1 0) (× 16 16))
(= (big 2 0) (× 2 (big 1 0))) (= (big 1 5) (+ (big 1 0) 5))
(< 255 (big 1 0)) (> (big 2 0) (big 1 255))
three digits: 256²
(= (big 1 0 0) (× (big 1 0) (big 1 0)))

As bits, (big 1 0) ends with the digits 00000001 00000000. Put the digits side by side and you have 256 in binary.
