Dimensions and Planck units

So far the message has only compared quantities of the same kind, such as two masses. To say how big something is, it needs units. The units used here are Planck units, built from three constants of nature: the speed of light c, the quantum of action ħ and the gravitational constant G. Each constant is introduced through the law it appears in, so its meaning, including any factor such as 2π, is fixed by the message itself.
