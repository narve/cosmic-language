isotopes

New: isotope and stable. Atoms of the same element can have different numbers of neutrons. (isotope hydrogen 1) is hydrogen with 1 neutron.

(= (count proton (isotope hydrogen 1)) 1)  (= (count neutron (isotope hydrogen 1)) 1)
(= (count proton (isotope helium 2)) 2)  (= (count neutron (isotope helium 2)) 2)
(∀ u elements (∀ v ℕ (= (count neutron (isotope u v)) v)))
(∀ u elements (∀ v ℕ (= (count proton (isotope u v)) (count proton u))))
