gravity and G

New: the domain bodies (things with mass), the functions gravity (the force of gravity between two bodies) and distance, and the constant G. Newton's law of gravitation is written out in full. It holds for bodies that move slowly and are not too heavy or close, which is the case the domain bodies stands for:

(observed (∀ u bodies (∀ v bodies
  (= (gravity u v) (÷ (× G (mass u) (mass v)) (^ (distance u v) 2))))))
