lists

New: list, length, item and count. A list is several values in a fixed order, written (list 4 7 9). The items are counted from 1.

(= (list 4 7 9) (list 4 7 9))
order matters
(≠ (list 1 2) (list 2 1))
(= (length (list 4 7 9)) 3)  (= (length (list 5)) 1)  (= (length (list)) 0)
(= (item (list 4 7 9) 1) 4)  (= (item (list 4 7 9) 2) 7)  (= (item (list 4 7 9) 3) 9)
lists can contain lists
(= (length (list (list 1 2) (list 3))) 2)
(= (item (list (list 1 2) (list 3)) 2) (list 3))

count tells how many times a value occurs:

(= (count 3 (list 3 1 3)) 2)  (= (count 1 (list 3 1 3)) 1)  (= (count 5 (list 3 1 3)) 0)
(= (count 7 (list 7 7 7 7)) 4)

And the general laws:

(∀ u ℕ (∀ v ℕ (= (length (list u v)) 2)))
(∀ u ℕ (∀ v ℕ (= (item (list u v) 1) u)))
the last item
(∀ u ℕ (∀ v ℕ (= (item (list u v) (length (list u v))) v)))
