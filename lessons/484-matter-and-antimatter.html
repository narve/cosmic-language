matter and antimatter

Every particle has a twin of opposite charge: its antiparticle. New: positron, the electron's twin, and antiparticle, which gives the twin of a particle. A particle without charge may be its own twin, as the photon is, or not, as the antineutrino of the lesson on spin is not: its twin, the neutrino, is not in this message.

; the atoms of the elements hold electrons, and no positrons

A world could be built of antiparticles instead: antiatoms, with positrons round antiprotons. Its light, its chemistry and its gravity would be exactly like ours, so nothing so far tells a reader which of the two it lives in. That matters for the lesson on spin: in such a world every decay there comes out reversed, and a reader would take left for right. The next lesson tells the two worlds apart.
