division and fractions

New symbol: ÷. It follows the same pattern as subtraction. First, only divisions that come out even:

(= (÷ 6 3) 2) (= (÷ 8 2) 4) (= (÷ 9 9) 1) (= (÷ 0 5) 0) (= (÷ 7 1) 7)
÷ undoes ×
(= (× (÷ 6 3) 3) 6)

Then divisions that do not come out even. No new symbol is needed: the result is simply written as a division, reduced to lowest terms.

(= (× (÷ 1 3) 3) 1) (= (+ (÷ 1 3) (÷ 1 3) (÷ 1 3)) 1)
the same value, in lowest terms
(= (÷ 2 6) (÷ 1 3)) (= (÷ 4 6) (÷ 2 3))
(= (+ (÷ 1 2) (÷ 1 3)) (÷ 5 6))
fractions lie between whole numbers
(< 0 (÷ 1 3) (÷ 1 2) 1)
(= (÷ (neg 6) 3) (neg 2)) (= (÷ 1 (neg 3)) (neg (÷ 1 3)))

Division by zero never appears in the message.
