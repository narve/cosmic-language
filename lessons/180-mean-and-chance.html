mean and chance

New: mean, chance and pairs. The mean of a list is the sum of its items divided by how many there are:


; the mean of 2, 4 and 9 is 5; the mean of a single item is that
; item
(= (mean (list 2 4 9)) 5)
(= (mean (list 7)) 7)

; the mean need not be a whole number: 7/2
(= (mean (list 1 2 3 4 5 6)) (÷ 7 2))

; what mean means: the sum divided by the length
(= (mean (list 2 4 9)) (÷ (apply + (list 2 4 9)) (length (list 2 4 9))))

The chance of a value in a list is the part of the list that is that value: how many of the items are that value, divided by the number of items. It is a proportion, nothing more.


; one of two items is true: chance 1/2
(= (chance true (list true false)) (÷ 1 2))
; one of six items is 6: chance 1/6
(= (chance 6 (list 1 2 3 4 5 6)) (÷ 1 6))
; two of three items are 3: chance 2/3
(= (chance 3 (list 3 1 3)) (÷ 2 3))
; 5 is not in the list: chance 0
(= (chance 5 (list 1 2 2)) 0)

; what chance means: the count divided by the length
(= (chance 3 (list 3 1 3)) (÷ (count 3 (list 3 1 3)) (length (list 3 1 3))))
; the chances of all the different items add up to 1
(= (+ (chance 1 (list 1 2 2)) (chance 2 (list 1 2 2))) 1)

pairs makes every pair of an item from one list with an item from another. The chance of a pair is the product of the two chances:


; every pair of an item from the first list with an item from the
; second
(= (pairs (list 1 2) (list 3 4)) (list (list 1 3) (list 1 4) (list 2 3) (list 2 4)))
; 3 items and 2 items give 6 pairs
(= (length (pairs (list 1 2 3) (list 4 5))) 6)

; two picks from true and false: the chance that both are true is
; 1/4 …
(= (chance (list true true) (pairs (list true false) (list true false))) (÷ 1 4))
; … which is the chance of one true times the chance of the other
(= (chance (list true true) (pairs (list true false) (list true false)))
   (× (chance true (list true false)) (chance true (list true false))))

; two picks from 1 to 6: the chance of two sixes is 1/36
(= (chance (list 6 6) (pairs (list 1 2 3 4 5 6) (list 1 2 3 4 5 6))) (÷ 1 36))
