molecules

New: molecules (a new type), and the functions atoms and bonds. Atoms other than the noble gases join into molecules. count tells how many atoms of an element a molecule contains, and counting goes on through the atoms to their particles:

water: two hydrogen and one oxygen
(= (count hydrogen water) 2)  (= (count oxygen water) 1)
(= (count proton water) 10)  (= (count electron water) 10)
(= (count carbon methane) 1)  (= (count hydrogen methane) 4)
(= (count nitrogen ammonia) 1)  (= (count hydrogen ammonia) 3)
(= (count carbon carbon-dioxide) 1)  (= (count oxygen carbon-dioxide) 2)

atoms gives a molecule's atoms as a list. bonds gives its bonds: each bond is a list of the positions of two atoms and the number of electron pairs they share.

(= (atoms water) (list oxygen hydrogen hydrogen))
the oxygen (1) shares one pair with each hydrogen (2 and 3)
(= (bonds water) (list (list 1 2 1) (list 1 3 1)))
(= (atoms carbon-dioxide) (list carbon oxygen oxygen))
the carbon shares two pairs with each oxygen
(= (bonds carbon-dioxide) (list (list 1 2 2) (list 1 3 2)))
(= (atoms methane) (list carbon hydrogen hydrogen hydrogen hydrogen))
(= (bonds methane) (list (list 1 2 1) (list 1 3 1) (list 1 4 1) (list 1 5 1)))
(= (atoms ammonia) (list nitrogen hydrogen hydrogen hydrogen))
(= (bonds ammonia) (list (list 1 2 1) (list 1 3 1) (list 1 4 1)))
the count of an element is its count in the list of atoms
(= (count hydrogen water) (count hydrogen (atoms water)))
