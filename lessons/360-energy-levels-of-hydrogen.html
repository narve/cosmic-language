the energy levels of hydrogen

New: the function level, the energy of a hydrogen atom in its 1st, 2nd, 3rd, … state. Again only ratios are given. First the mathematics: if the levels go as 1 − 1/n², the steps between them have exact ratios.

; the steps between 1, 1/4 and 1/9 (that is 1/1², 1/2² and 1/3²)
(= (− 1 (÷ 1 4)) (÷ 3 4))  (= (− 1 (÷ 1 9)) (÷ 8 9))  (= (− (÷ 1 4) (÷ 1 9)) (÷ 5 36))

; the step from 1 to 2, divided by the step from 2 to 3: 3/4 ÷ 5/36 = 27/5
(= (÷ (− 1 (÷ 1 4)) (− (÷ 1 4) (÷ 1 9))) (÷ 27 5))
; the step from 1 to 3, divided by the step from 1 to 2: 8/9 ÷ 3/4 = 32/27
(= (÷ (− 1 (÷ 1 9)) (− 1 (÷ 1 4))) (÷ 32 27))

Then the measurements, which agree with these ratios:

; each level lies above the one before
(observed (< (level hydrogen 1) (level hydrogen 2) (level hydrogen 3)))

; (level 2 − level 1) ÷ (level 3 − level 2) is between 5.39 and 5.41; 27/5 is 5.4
; (539 is written (big 2 27) and 541 is (big 2 29))
(observed (< (÷ (big 2 27) 100)
              (÷ (− (level hydrogen 2) (level hydrogen 1))
                 (− (level hydrogen 3) (level hydrogen 2)))
              (÷ (big 2 29) 100)))

; (level 3 − level 1) ÷ (level 2 − level 1) is between 1.18 and 1.19; 32/27 is about 1.185
(observed (< (÷ 118 100)
              (÷ (− (level hydrogen 3) (level hydrogen 1))
                 (− (level hydrogen 2) (level hydrogen 1)))
              (÷ 119 100)))

The first range contains 27/5, the second 32/27. These ratios are the pattern every spectroscopist finds first in the light from hydrogen.
