addition and nesting

New symbol: +. This is the first lesson where groups appear inside groups.

(= (+ 0 0) 0) (= (+ 1 1) 2) (= (+ 2 3) 5) (= (+ 3 2) 5)
adding 0 changes nothing
(= (+ 1 0) 1) (= (+ 0 7) 7)
up to the largest number in a chunk
(= (+ 254 1) 255)
+ inside other relations
(< (+ 2 2) 5) (≠ (+ 2 2) 5)
+ adds any number of terms
(= (+ 1 2 3) 6) (= (+ 1 1 1 1) 4) (= (+ 2 0 3 0) 5)
grouping does not matter
(= (+ 1 2 3) (+ (+ 1 2) 3) (+ 1 (+ 2 3)))
