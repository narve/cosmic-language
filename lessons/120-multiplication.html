multiplication

New symbol: ×.

; the multiples of 3
(= (× 3 1) 3)
(= (× 3 2) 6)
(= (× 3 3) 9)
(= (× 3 4) 12)
(= (× 3 5) 15)

; multiplying adds the same number again and again: 2 × 3 = 3 + 3
(= (× 2 3) (+ 3 3) 6)
(= (× 3 2) (+ 2 2 2) 6)
(= (× 4 7) (+ 7 7 7 7) 28)

; the order does not matter; times 0 gives 0, times 1 changes
; nothing
(= (× 2 3) 6)
(= (× 3 2) 6)
(= (× 0 5) 0)
(= (× 1 9) 9)

; nesting two levels deep: 2 × (1 + 2) = 6
(= (× 2 (+ 1 2)) 6)

; × multiplies any number of factors
(= (× 2 3 4) 24)
(= (× 2 2 2 2) 16)
(= (× 5 1 1) 5)

; grouping does not matter
(= (× 2 3 4) (× (× 2 3) 4) (× 2 (× 3 4)))

; up to the largest number in a chunk, and one product beyond it: 16
; × 16 is more than 255
(= (× 15 15) 225)
(= (× 15 17) 255)
(> (× 16 16) 255)

The last example is a product that is larger than any single number token. The next lesson shows how to write it.
