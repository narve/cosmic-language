Chunks: small numbers

After the beacon, the bitstream is divided into chunks of 8 bits. Each chunk is simply a number in binary, with the most significant bit first, so a chunk holds 0–255. (The chunk size is a constant in the format and may change later.)

Example

00000101   = 5
11111111   = 255, the largest number that fits in one chunk

Larger numbers are not needed at the start. They come later as expressions, once the message has taught arithmetic (see the lesson on large numbers).
