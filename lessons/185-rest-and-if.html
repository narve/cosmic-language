rest and if

New: rest, a list without its first item, and if, which picks one of two values. With item, rest lets a list be taken apart one item at a time: the first item, then the rest.


(= (rest (list 4 7 9)) (list 7 9))
(= (rest (list 7 9)) (list 9))
(= (rest (list 9)) (list))

(= (length (rest (list 4 7 9))) 2)
(∀ u ℕ (∀ v ℕ (= (rest (list u v)) (list v))))
(∀ u ℕ (∀ v ℕ (∀ w ℕ (= (item (rest (list u v w)) 2) (item (list u v w) 3)))))

rest is never applied to an empty list.

(if a b c) is b when a is true, and c when a is false:


(= (if true 1 2) 1)
(= (if false 1 2) 2)

(= (if (< 2 3) 5 7) 5)
(= (if (not (< 2 3)) 5 7) 7)
