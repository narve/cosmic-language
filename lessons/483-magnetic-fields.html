magnetic fields

A current going round in a ring makes a magnetic field. New: the domain rings (round loops of wire carrying a steady current), radius, circulates (the current goes round from one direction toward another), and field-direction and field-strength, the direction and strength of the magnetic field at the centre of the ring.


; a ring has a radius, a length, and a current, a charge per time
(∀ u rings (= (dim (radius u)) L))
(∀ u rings (> (radius u) 0))
(∀ u rings (= (dim (current u)) (÷ Q T)))

; the strength at the centre: 2π × km × current ÷ radius
(observed (∀ u rings (= (field-strength u) (÷ (× 2 π km (abs (current u))) (radius u)))))
; its dimension: mass ÷ (charge × time)
(∀ u rings (= (dim (field-strength u)) (÷ M (× Q T))))
; two rings of the same size: twice the current gives twice the
; field
(∀ u rings (∀ v rings
  (implies (and (= (radius u) (radius v)) (= (abs (current u)) (× 2 (abs (current v)))))
           (= (field-strength u) (× 2 (field-strength v))))))

The field points along the axis of the ring, and which way it points is given by cross, just as for spin. This is a second way to tie the hand of cross to something in the world. But it does not tell left from right by itself: in a mirror, the laws of magnetism come out the same. Only the decays in the lesson on spin, and the kaon in the next lesson, do that.


; a current going round from v toward w makes a field along the
; cross of v and w
(∀ u rings (∀ v directions (∀ w directions
  (implies (and (= (dot v w) 0) (circulates u v w))
           (= (field-direction u) (cross v w))))))
; for example: going round from (1 0 0) toward (0 1 0), the field is
; along (0 0 1)
(∀ u rings (implies (circulates u (list 1 0 0) (list 0 1 0)) (= (field-direction u) (list 0 0 1))))
; a current that goes round from v toward w does not go round from w
; toward v
(∀ u rings (∀ v directions (∀ w directions (implies (circulates u v w) (not (circulates u w v))))))

; a spinning particle is a tiny ring: its own field points along its
; spin axis, or against it
(observed (= (dot (field-direction proton) (spin-axis proton)) 1))
(observed (= (dot (field-direction electron) (spin-axis electron)) (neg 1)))
(observed (= (dot (field-direction neutron) (spin-axis neutron)) (neg 1)))
