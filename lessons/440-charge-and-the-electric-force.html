charge and the electric force

New: the dimension Q (charge), the function electric (the electric force between two bodies) and the constant ke. The lesson on particles compared charges; now they get a dimension and a law. A positive force pushes apart, a negative one pulls together.

(= (dim (charge proton)) Q)
(observed (∀ u bodies (∀ v bodies
  (= (electric u v) (÷ (× ke (charge u) (charge v)) (^ (distance u v) 2))))))
(= (dim ke) (÷ (× M (^ L 3)) (× (^ T 2) (^ Q 2))))

Because the law is written out, the next number does not depend on how charge is measured. It is the fine-structure constant, which sets the strength of the electric force:

between 1/138 and 1/137
(observed (< (÷ 1 138) (÷ (× ke (^ (charge proton) 2)) (× ħ c)) (÷ 1 137)))
between a proton and an electron, the electric force is about 2.3 × 10³⁹ times the force of gravity
(observed (< (^ 2 130) (÷ (abs (electric proton electron)) (gravity proton electron)) (^ 2 131)))
