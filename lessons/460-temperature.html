temperature

New: the dimension Θ (temperature), the constant kB, the domains gases (thin gases of single atoms) and black-bodies (bodies that absorb all light falling on them), and the functions temperature, kinetic (the average energy of motion of a gas's particles), radiance (the power a body radiates per area) and triple-point. Again kB is introduced through a law:

the particles of a gas move with an average energy of 3/2 kB × its temperature
(observed (∀ u gases (= (kinetic u) (× (÷ 3 2) kB (temperature u)))))
(= (dim kB) (÷ (× M (^ L 2)) (× (^ T 2) Θ)))
(∀ u gases (= (dim (temperature u)) Θ))
an energy
(∀ u gases (= (dim (kinetic u)) (÷ (× M (^ L 2)) (^ T 2))))
the same law, read as what temperature is
(observed (∀ u gases (= (temperature u) (÷ (× 2 (kinetic u)) (× 3 kB)))))

A black body radiates power that grows with the fourth power of its temperature. The factor in front is made only of constants the message has already introduced, and π²/60 is the signature of the spectrum of thermal light:

(observed (∀ u black-bodies
  (= (radiance u) (× (÷ (^ π 2) 60) (÷ (^ kB 4) (× (^ ħ 3) (^ c 2))) (^ (temperature u) 4)))))
power per area: mass ÷ time³
(∀ u black-bodies (= (dim (radiance u)) (÷ M (^ T 3))))
(∀ u black-bodies (= (dim (temperature u)) Θ))
twice the temperature: 16 times the power
(observed (∀ u black-bodies (∀ v black-bodies
  (implies (= (temperature u) (× 2 (temperature v))) (= (radiance u) (× 16 (radiance v)))))))

The Planck temperature completes the Planck units:

(= (^ planck-temperature 2) (÷ (× ħ (^ c 5)) (× G (^ kB 2))))
(= (dim planck-temperature) Θ)
at the Planck temperature, the thermal energy is the energy of the Planck mass
(= (× kB planck-temperature) (× planck-mass (^ c 2)))

A temperature that is the same everywhere: water's triple point, where ice, liquid water and vapour exist together. Its thermal energy is compared with hydrogen's step from level 1 to level 2:
