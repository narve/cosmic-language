The message ends with what the tournament shows, and with the reason for the strategy we have stated. Two players who keep the peace each lose less than two players at war. The two strategies that defect first end with the highest losses, and the four that never defect first all end below them. And tit-for-tat, which opens in peace, answers in kind and forgives as soon as the other returns to peace, loses less than every other strategy. That is the last statement of the message.


; two at peace lose less, each, than two at war: 765 against 1275
