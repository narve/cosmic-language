the Sun

One particular star, and one body that goes round it. New: the constants sun and earth. This lesson can be left out of a message: no other lesson uses what it introduces.

; the Sun is a star, and the Earth is a body
(∃ u stars (= u sun))
(∃ u bodies (= u earth))

; the Sun's mass: between 2¹⁸⁹ and 2¹⁹⁰ proton masses
(observed (< (^ 2 189) (÷ (mass sun) (mass proton)) (^ 2 190)))
; its radius: between 2¹⁴⁴ and 2¹⁴⁵ Planck lengths
(observed (< (^ 2 144) (÷ (radius sun) planck-length) (^ 2 145)))
; its temperature: between 21 and 22 times the triple point of water
(observed (< 21 (÷ (temperature sun) (triple-point water)) 22))
; its luminosity: between 2⁻⁸⁷ and 2⁻⁸⁶ of the Planck unit of power,
; c⁵ ÷ G
(observed (< (÷ 1 (^ 2 87)) (÷ (luminosity sun) (÷ (^ c 5) G)) (÷ 1 (^ 2 86))))
; at its surface, between 1/16 and 1/8 helium atom for every
; hydrogen atom
(observed (< (÷ 1 (^ 2 4)) (÷ (count helium sun) (count hydrogen sun)) (÷ 1 (^ 2 3))))

The Earth goes round the Sun. Its orbit and its mass are given in units that any reader has: Planck lengths, Planck times and proton masses.

; the Earth orbits the Sun
(observed (orbits earth sun))
; the size of its orbit: between 2¹⁵² and 2¹⁵³ Planck lengths
(observed (< (^ 2 152) (÷ (axis earth sun) planck-length) (^ 2 153)))
; one round takes between 2¹⁶⁸ and 2¹⁶⁹ Planck times
(observed (< (^ 2 168) (÷ (period earth sun) planck-time) (^ 2 169)))
; the Earth's mass: between 2¹⁷¹ and 2¹⁷² proton masses
(observed (< (^ 2 171) (÷ (mass earth) (mass proton)) (^ 2 172)))

Sources: NASA Sun and Earth fact sheets (2024), CODATA 2022 (the Planck units and the proton mass), BIPM (water's triple point). Details in the specification, §8.4.
