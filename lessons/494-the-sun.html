the Sun

One particular star, and one body that goes round it. New: the constants sun and earth. This lesson can be left out of a message: no other lesson uses what it introduces.

; the Sun is a star, and the Earth is a body
(∃ u stars (= u sun))
(∃ u bodies (= u earth))

; the Sun's mass: between 2¹⁸⁹ and 2¹⁹⁰ proton masses
(observed (< (^ 2 189) (÷ (mass sun) (mass proton)) (^ 2 190)))
; its radius: between 2¹⁴⁴ and 2¹⁴⁵ Planck lengths
(observed (< (^ 2 144) (÷ (radius sun) planck-length) (^ 2 145)))
; its temperature: between 21 and 22 times the triple point of water
(observed (< 21 (÷ (temperature sun) (triple-point water)) 22))
; its luminosity: between 2⁻⁸⁷ and 2⁻⁸⁶ of the Planck unit of power, c⁵ ÷ G
(observed (< (÷ 1 (^ 2 87)) (÷ (luminosity sun) (÷ (^ c 5) G)) (÷ 1 (^ 2 86))))
; at its surface, between 1/16 and 1/8 helium atom for every hydrogen atom
(observed (< (÷ 1 (^ 2 4)) (÷ (count helium sun) (count hydrogen sun)) (÷ 1 (^ 2 3))))

The Earth goes round the Sun. Its orbit is given against the Sun itself and against the hydrogen line, which a receiver can measure.

; the Earth orbits the Sun
(observed (orbits earth sun))
; the size of its orbit: between 214 and 216 times the Sun's radius
(observed (< 214 (÷ (axis earth sun) (radius sun)) 216))
; one round takes between 2⁵⁵ and 2⁵⁶ periods of the hydrogen line
(observed (< (^ 2 55) (× (period earth sun) (frequency hydrogen-line)) (^ 2 56)))
; the Sun is between 2¹⁸ and 2¹⁹ times heavier than the Earth
(observed (< (^ 2 18) (÷ (mass sun) (mass earth)) (^ 2 19)))

Sources: NASA Sun and Earth fact sheets (2024), CODATA 2022, JPL catalog (the 21 cm line), BIPM (water's triple point). Details in the specification, §8.4.
