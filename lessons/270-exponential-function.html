the exponential function

Some numbers are not roots of any polynomial with whole-number coefficients. The two most important ones, e and π, cannot be described with the tools so far. They need one more building block: the exponential function.

New symbol: exp. After a few of its values, it is introduced by two properties that together single out exactly one function on ℝ:

; three values first: exp of 0 is 1, and exp of 1 is between 2 and 3
(= (exp 0) 1)  (> (exp 1) 2)  (< (exp 1) 3)
; always above 0
(∀ u ℝ (> (exp u) 0))

; the first property: it turns sums into products
(∀ u ℝ (∀ v ℝ (= (exp (+ u v)) (× (exp u) (exp v)))))
; the second property: it is never below 1 + u
(∀ u ℝ (≥ (exp u) (+ 1 u)))

On ℂ, exp has the same product rule. A bound close to 0 rules out every other candidate:

; the same product rule
(∀ u ℂ (∀ v ℂ (= (exp (+ u v)) (× (exp u) (exp v)))))

; close to 0, exp of u is close to 1 + u: where |u| ≤ 1, they differ by at most |u|²
(∀ u ℂ (implies (≤ (abs u) 1)
                   (≤ (abs (− (− (exp u) 1) u)) (^ (abs u) 2))))
