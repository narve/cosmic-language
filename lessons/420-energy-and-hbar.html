energy and ħ

New: the function energy, the constant ħ, and emitted: the photon a hydrogen atom emits when it falls from one level to a lower one.

the energy of a photon is ħ × 2π × its frequency
(observed (∀ u photons (= (energy u) (× ħ 2 π (frequency u)))))
(= (dim ħ) (÷ (× M (^ L 2)) T))
falling from level 2 to level 1, hydrogen emits a photon carrying the difference,
apart from a tiny share taken by the recoil of the atom
(observed (< (÷ 99 100)
              (÷ (energy (emitted hydrogen 2 1)) (− (level hydrogen 2) (level hydrogen 1)))
              1))

This ties the energy levels of hydrogen to light that can be measured.
