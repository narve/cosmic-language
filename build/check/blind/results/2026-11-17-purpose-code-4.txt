## 4. Purpose

**Stated.** Mutual cooperation beats mutual defection. A defector outscores any single partner (259 against 254; 1275 against 0). Even so, the strategy that cooperates first and then returns what it receives has the highest total in the field.

**Inferred.** The message is an argument for reciprocal cooperation, addressed to a stranger the sender expects to deal with more than once. The evidence:
- it is the conclusion, and nothing follows it;
- it is the only non-mathematical, non-physical subject, and takes a seventh of the message;
- the message is candid that defection wins pairwise, which reads as "we know the temptation, and here is why not";
- the mathematics and physics serve to build the language and to show the sender is competent and truthful, since everything is checkable.

**Guess.** The sender is announcing its own policy: open cooperatively, answer in kind. It hopes the receiver adopts the same, most plausibly by answering. The four formula-only molecules and water at about 273 K may be a self-description of the sender's chemistry.
